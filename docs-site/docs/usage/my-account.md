# My Account

Open your name in the navigation bar and choose **My account**. These settings
belong to your login, not to the school.

## Appearance

Choose a theme, preview it immediately, then select **Save appearance**:

| Theme | Appearance |
| --- | --- |
| System | Follows your device's light or dark preference. This is the default for staff accounts. |
| Light | Light backgrounds, regardless of your device setting. |
| Dark | Soft charcoal backgrounds with lighter text and controls. |

Your saved choice follows your account when you sign in on another device.
Book covers keep their original colors and printed labels stay black on white.

:::info Shared circulation computer
After signing out, the public catalog and circulation desk use Light. Your
personal preference does not change the next borrower's screen.
:::

## Daily use

Choose your **Start page after login**: Overview (the default), Circulation, or
Catalog. Select **Save preferences** to keep your choice. A normal sign-in opens
that page; signing in to access a specific protected page still takes you to
the page you requested.

**Table spacing** offers Comfortable (the default) and Compact. Compact reduces
the space around table cells without reducing text size or action buttons.
It applies to tables while you are signed in, not to circulation controls or
printed pages. Signed-out visitors always get Comfortable spacing.

These preferences follow your account across devices and do not change another
staff member's settings.

## Password and profile

To change your password, enter your current password and your new password twice,
then select **Change password**. You stay signed in after changing it.

Your profile shows your name, generated Borrower ID, separate login username,
email, and access level.
A system administrator manages those details through
[Staff Accounts](./staff-accounts.md).

## School settings

For the school name, loan duration, classes, and employee positions, open
**Library > School settings** instead. Those settings affect the whole school.
See [School Setup](../getting-started/school-setup.md).
